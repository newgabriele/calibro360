#### Why Calibro360™ Exists

Throughout decades of troubleshooting high-throughput CSTR digesters, managing VFA/TIC ratios, and tackling persistent $H_2S$ challenges, a clear bottleneck became evident: plant operators were often reacting to chemical imbalances rather than predicting them. 

Traditional dosing methods for desulfurization (such as crude iron chlorides) often introduce secondary operational risks. By shifting the focus to precise stoichiometric modeling—utilizing targeted iron oxides/hydroxides for $H_2S$ mitigation and magnetite ($Fe_3O_4$) for Direct Interspecies Electron Transfer (DIET)—plant efficiency can be radically transformed.

**Calibro360™ bridges this exact gap.** 

By combining decades of biochemical fieldwork with modern Python-driven architecture, Streamlit automation, and applied AI, Calibro360™ empowers plant managers, engineers, and developers with real-time, data-driven predictive control. 

*Lower OPEX. Maximum biological stability. Zero guesswork.*
